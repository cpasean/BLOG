Every moment is a part of my life, and in the end, the accumulation becomes a story of a completed life, so I must feel happy even at the moment of doing a boring or rather routine job.

It is only possible if I am able to vividly imagine and appreciate my long-term goal to enlighten the meaning of life in terms of its value. We shouldn't too much think of that or be hard on ourselves but should communicate deeply with ourselves for its precious value before revising our life roadmap.

I love myself from the bottom of my heart and I'm willing to say to myself that 'I am super fine. and I am really happy now.'
