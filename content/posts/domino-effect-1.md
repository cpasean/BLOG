Domino effect - When an action item happens to be mixed up with insignificant things, the result has an adverse effect on the next item, and nothing is achieved throughout the day. If this happens for days in a row, I might fall into a vicious cycle that makes me depressed.

Let's get over the past and be possessed by my soul and finally enjoy the time travel.
