People's minds are expressed by language. We can touch or change others with the language of the pure spirit. As long as people sincerely share their hearts, notions such as a theory or logic are just ostensibly added. We don't just admire good announcers or people having a strong sense of their word.

Only by finding a naive sincerity from one another, we eventually open our hearts to them even though they have different perspectives. Language is the odor that comes from someone's heart. The expression of language tells us whether the smell is fragrance or stinkiness. Fortune is always with aroma.

Some people stay positive and enabled to make consistent evolution forming an initiative to achieve prosperity. We call them leaders. Let's be a leader of ourselves for another prosperous day.
