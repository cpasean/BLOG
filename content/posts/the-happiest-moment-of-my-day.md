The happiest moment of my day was when I stayed at a beautiful cafe on the way home from work. As I read, I preferred to sit by the window where I could gaze out through a coated window, unnoticed by people. It was the happiest moment of the day and it's still the same now.

It would be really important to ask myself what makes me happy and that's an undeniable question. Don't be nervous. Lead the day happily and positively without being led.
