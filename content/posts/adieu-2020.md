We are now heading towards the end of the year. The pandemic has held me back from going out of the country since the beginning of this year after I finally passed my CPA exam.

On the contrary, I used this opportunity to remind myself of my principles in life and to always remember that I could get through anything in life. Indeed, this year has helped me achieved a lot of unaccomplished goals such as waking up at 4:30 am, improving my English pronunciation skills, finding short-term objectives as a public accountant, and of course, getting a positive result for my US CPA exam.

I want to thank my guardian angel for guiding me to achieve these accomplishments.
