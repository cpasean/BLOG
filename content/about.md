## Experience

Sean Yeon has diverse work experience spanning several industries. Sean started his career at Sony Electronics in 2005, where he worked as a Project Manager until 2009. Sean then moved on to Broadcom Inc. in 2010, where he served as a Senior Project Manager for seven years. In 2019, he joined KPMG as a Tax Associate and worked there until 2020. After that, he worked at QED as an Algorithmic Stock Trading Bot Developer. Currently, he is employed at Nonghyup Bank as the Team Head of Accounting and Control, where he is responsible for overseeing various banking operations and ensuring compliance with regulatory standards.

## Education

Sean Yeon has a diverse educational background with a focus on business and financial analysis. In 2017, Sean obtained a Master's degree in Business Administration and Management from Yonsei University. This was followed by a Graduate Certificate in Accounting from Zicklin School of Business in 2018. In terms of higher education, Sean pursued a Master of Science degree with a specialization in Financial Analysis at Zicklin School of Business, which completed in 2023.

Alongside his formal education, Sean has also demonstrated a commitment to continuous learning and professional development. Sean completed several Nanodegree programs offered by Udacity. In 2021, he obtained a Data Analyst Nanodegree, focusing on practical statistics, data wrangling, and data visualization. The following year, he gained expertise in machine learning through the Machine Learning Nanodegree program, which covered supervised learning, deep learning (with TensorFlow), and unsupervised learning. In 2022, Sean further expanded his knowledge in the field by completing a Deep Learning Nanodegree, which delved into convolutional neural networks, RNNs and transformers, and generative adversarial networks.
